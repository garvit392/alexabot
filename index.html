Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 18, 2026

This policy explains what data Alexa collects, why, how long we keep it, and your choices.

1. Data We Collect

1.1 From Discord (via the bot)

  • Music & Playlists — Track titles, URLs, and saved playlists stored against your Discord user ID.
  • Moderation — Warnings and moderation cases (user ID, moderator ID, reason, timestamp).
  • General / Profiles — Profile bios, badges, AFK status, and leaderboard data.
  • Settings — Server configuration (prefix, DJ role, announce channel, whitelist).
  • Customize — Per-server bot customization data (avatar, banner, nickname, bio).

1.2 From Discord OAuth2 Login (website)

When you click "Login with Discord", we receive your Discord user ID, username, global display name, and avatar via the identify scope. We do NOT request your email, messages, friends list, or server list. The access token is stored only in your browser's memory and never sent to our servers.

1.3 Operational Logs

Standard request logs (IP, timestamp, path, status code) kept for up to 14 days then deleted.

2. How Long We Keep Data

  • Per-server configuration — until the server removes Alexa or you request deletion.
  • Playlists and liked songs — until you delete them or request removal.
  • Moderation cases — until cleared by an admin or bot removal.
  • Operational logs — up to 14 days.
  • OAuth tokens — kept only in your browser session, never on our servers.

3. How We Use Your Data

To deliver music, playlist, moderation, and other features. To keep the bot secure and prevent abuse. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

4. Who We Share Data With

  • Discord — Subject to Discord's Privacy Policy.
  • Lavalink nodes — Audio query data passes through Lavalink infrastructure.
  • Infrastructure providers — Host the bot on our behalf.

5. Your Rights & Choices

  • Request data deletion via the support server.
  • Revoke OAuth at any time from Discord's Authorised Apps settings.
  • Kick Alexa from your server to stop all event processing.
  • Use +playlist-delete to permanently remove your playlists.

6. Children

Alexa is not directed at children under Discord's minimum age for your jurisdiction.

7. Security

We use HTTPS, modern encryption, and least-privilege practices.

8. Changes

Material changes will be announced in the support server and reflected in the updated date above.

9. Contact

Reach us in the official support server.
